Delete Your DobiKoop Account

Last updated: 27 September 2026

If you wish to delete your DobiKoop account and associated data, you can do so using either of the methods below.

Option 1: Delete In-App

  1. Open the DobiKoop app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you're unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us directly:

Email: ainbsyrhhh@gmail.com
Subject: DobiKoop Account Deletion Request
Include: Your registered email address and/or student ID

We will process your request within 7 business days and confirm via email once completed.

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your name, student ID, phone number, and email address
  • Your account credentials (login information)
  • Your profile information

What May Be Retained

For record-keeping, legal, and financial reporting purposes, the following may be retained even after account deletion:

  • Booking and transaction history (anonymized, not linked to your personal identity)
  • Payment receipts required for cooperative financial audits
