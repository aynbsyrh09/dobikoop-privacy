Request Account Deletion

What Gets Deleted

  • Your name, student ID, phone number, and email address

What May Be Retained

For record-keeping and financial audit purposes, the following may be retained (anonymized, not linked to your identity):

  • Payment receipts required for cooperative financial reporting
